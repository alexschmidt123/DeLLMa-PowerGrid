Authoritative and market data (only folder besides simulation/ under data/powergrid/)

Put here
- Government / regulator / ISO–style open data you are allowed to redistribute (EIA, DOE, FERC, etc.).
- Company or grid–operator **market reports**, briefings, or statistics you have rights to use, each with SOURCES.txt:
  provider, document name, URL or filing id, date, license.

Do not put Pandapower, MATLAB, or Simulink outputs here — use ../simulation/pandapower/ or ../simulation/matlab_simulink/.
